Teams and colours

Each age group has its own colour; its B team uses a lighter shade of it. The short code is what appears on each match.

Opponent clubs

Kazakhstan clubs: one per line. They are sorted A–Z automatically and appear in the Opponent list.

Logos: tap “Logo” next to a club and pick its crest image (PNG or JPG). Until then a coloured badge with its initials is shown.

Competitions and logos

Upload each competition's logo; it appears on every match of that competition. Until there's a logo, the short code is shown.
